Chapter one

Treehouse to the Moon

Every night, June and her little sister Pip say goodnight to the Moon from their treehouse in the old oak. And every night, Pip asks the same question: “What do you think it’s like up there?”

Tonight they’re going to find out. For a whole year they saved every balloon from every birthday party. Their best friend Michelle is going to be so jealous. Now fifty balloons are tied to the roof, the cookies are packed, and dinner isn’t until seven.
