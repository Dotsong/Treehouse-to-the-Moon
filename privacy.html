Treehouse to the Moon

Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

The short version

Treehouse to the Moon doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game keeps on your device

The game remembers a few things so they are still there the next time you play: which science facts you've found, how many trips you've taken, whether you've met Lumi, and the text size you picked. That is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent anywhere. Settings → Restart progress clears it, and deleting the app deletes it.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It doesn't show ads or offer in-app purchases.
  • It doesn't use analytics, crash reporting or advertising services.
  • It doesn't link to other websites or apps.
  • The app works fully offline and doesn't send anything over the internet.

Children

The game is made for children and families. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

The web version

If you play in a web browser instead of the app, the page downloads its 3D engine from cdnjs and its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, those services see your device's internet address when they send the files. The game itself still collects nothing.

Treehouse to the Moon is an independent game.
