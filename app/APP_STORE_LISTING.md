## Description

Every night, June and her little sister Pip say goodnight to the Moon from their treehouse. Tonight, they're going to visit it.

Treehouse to the Moon is a Slowdown Storytime game: a slow, dreamy 3D story made for reading aloud with your children, with nothing to beat and nowhere to hurry. Run the pre-flight checklist with the girls, count down to liftoff, and watch their balloon-lifted treehouse drift up past birds and airplanes, through a big fluffy cloud, and out into space.

ON THE WAY UP
• Float over farms, forests, rivers, a little town and a twinkling city
• Share cookie crumbs with a little bird that stops on the railing
• Rise through every layer of the sky, from the clouds to outer space
• Wave at a hot-air balloon and friendly UFOs
• Make wishes on shooting stars

ON THE MOON
• Climb down the rope ladder and leave the first footprints
• Build moon castles and rock towers, draw in the dust, and make moon angels
• Play extra-long, low-gravity hopscotch
• Collect shiny moon rocks for a souvenir bucket
• Meet Lumi, a real moon girl who has been hiding behind a boulder

HOME FOR DINNER
• Float home under the stars as the town lights come on
• Mom and Dad are waiting on the lawn with a lantern, and the spaghetti is almost ready

A FACT BOOK TO FILL
Little book tags float over things along the way. Tap one to read a real science fact about balloons, clouds, the layers of the atmosphere, stars, the Moon and more, for older kids and grown-ups alike. There are 42 to collect.

MADE FOR CALM
• Gentle music made just for the trip
• Funny, sweet conversations between the sisters, and a storybook narrator
• Pause any time, and make the text bigger in Settings
• The girls remember earlier trips, and so does Lumi
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and no data collected
• Works with no internet connection
