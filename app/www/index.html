SlowdownStorytime

Story games made for reading aloud with your children.

Read the words together as the story drifts along. There’s nothing to win and no hurry, and you can pause at any time. Little book tags along the way open real science facts, for older kids and grown-ups alike.

Best with the sound on

How to play

  • The treehouse floats on its own. Sit back and read the girls’ words aloud as they appear.
  • Drag to look around, and pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Tap a book tag to open a real fact. Some only show while a bird, plane or UFO is about, so keep an eye out.
  • On the Moon, stay as long as you like. Tap Float home for dinner when it’s time to go.
  • Tap the pause button any time. The book under it holds every fact you’ve found, and Settings changes the text size and the music.

Chapter one

Treehouse to the Moon

Every night, June and her little sister Pip say goodnight to the Moon from their treehouse in the old oak. And every night, Pip asks the same question: “What do you think it’s like up there?”

Tonight they’re going to find out. For a whole year they saved every balloon from every birthday party. Now fifty of them are tied to the roof, the cookies are packed, and dinner isn’t until seven.
